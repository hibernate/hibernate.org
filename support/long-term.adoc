= Long-Term Support
:awestruct-layout: support-standard

The link:/support/maintenance-policy[maintenance] of Hibernate projects focuses on the latest series.

Maintenance of that series ends or switches to a limited mode as soon as a newer series gets released.

Application developers requiring longer support windows and/or contractually binding guarantees
should look for long-term support.
